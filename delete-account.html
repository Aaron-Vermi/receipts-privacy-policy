Receipts — Delete Your Account

Effective date: 29.08.2026

Receipts (published by WebUp - Vermeulen und Kupferschmid GbR) lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Receipts and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately — there is no waiting period, and no further action is needed from you.

Option 2 — By email (if you can't sign in, or no longer have the app)

Email contact@webupsolutions.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". Include the email address your account uses if it differs from the sending address. We process these requests manually and will confirm once your account has been deleted, normally within 5 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes, in full:

  • Your account credentials and profile (display name, profile photo)
  • Your tracked apps, categories, and daily time limit
  • Your daily and per-app usage history and coarse usage windows
  • Your accountability partnership, and all unlock requests, approvals/denials, self-unlock events, and nudges tied to it
  • Your notification and privacy settings, and any registered push-notification device

If you have an active accountability partner, they lose access to your data immediately as part of this — the partnership itself is deleted, not just your side of it.

What's retained, and for how long

Nothing is retained beyond the deletion request itself. Deletion is immediate and permanent in our live database. Routine infrastructure backups are cycled out and fully purged on their normal rotation, rather than being retroactively edited — nothing from a deleted account is ever restored or made accessible again during that window.
