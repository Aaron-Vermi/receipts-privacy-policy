Receipts — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31.08.2026

What Receipts is

Receipts is screen time accountability shared with one invited partner. It measures how long you spend in the apps you choose to track — never what you do inside them.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and a display name you choose, via Supabase Authentication. If you set a profile picture, it's stored in a private storage bucket tied only to your account.

Usage data. The apps you've selected to track, your daily time limit, your daily and per-app usage totals, and coarse three-hour usage windows — not a minute-by-minute activity log.

Accountability activity. Requests for extra time you send your partner, their approvals or denials, self-unlock events, and preset nudge messages sent between you and your partner. Nudges are chosen from a fixed set of preset messages — Receipts does not support free-text messaging between partners.

Device and notification data. An app-generated installation identifier and an Expo push-notification token, used only to deliver notifications to your own device. This is not a hardware identifier.

Permission status. Whether Android permissions Receipts needs (such as Usage Access and, if enabled, Accessibility) are currently granted — not the underlying data those permissions could technically expose.

Information we never collect

Message content, posts, or anything you type; screenshots or screen recordings; search terms or browsing history; location; the content shown on your screen or in your notifications; keystrokes; or a precise, continuous timeline of your device activity.

On Android, the component that detects when a tracked app is in the foreground reads only which app that is, and is configured (canRetrieveWindowContent="false") so that it is technically unable to read screen content, view hierarchies, or typed text.

How your information is shared

With your accountability partner — your display name, optional profile picture, and accountability actions involving that partner may be visible to them. Most aggregate sharing categories start enabled and can be turned off in Settings → What your partner sees. Daily totals and the per-app breakdown are shared only when both switches are on. Approvals and denials remain visible to both participants as part of the request they resolved. Removing the partnership stops access to your remaining data immediately.

With service providers who process data on our behalf — Supabase (hosting, database, authentication, file storage) and Expo's push notification service, which relays notifications to Android's and iOS's own push systems. These providers act under their own security and confidentiality commitments and do not use your data for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Receipts has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data retention

We retain your account and accountability data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account — in-app, from Settings → Delete account — your data is deleted immediately and unconditionally: there is no separate retention or grace period for usage summaries, request history, or any other account data. A residual copy may exist briefly in routine infrastructure backups before being purged, consistent with standard backup practices.

Your choices and rights

You can change or revoke the optional aggregate sharing categories at any time in Settings. Profile identity and accountability actions involving both participants work as described above rather than as independent sharing switches. You can remove a partnership to stop partner access immediately.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account, in-app, with no manual request needed. For access or correction requests, or any other question about your data, contact us at contact@webupsolutions.de.

Children's privacy

Receipts is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Data at rest is stored in Supabase's managed Postgres infrastructure with row-level security policies that restrict every table to its owner and, where explicitly enabled, an active accountability partner.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes, and for material changes, notify you in-app.
