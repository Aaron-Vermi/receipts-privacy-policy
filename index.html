Receipts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Receipts is an accountability app: it measures how long you spend in the apps you choose to track, and shares that with an accountability partner you invite. It does not read what you do in those apps. This policy explains exactly what is collected, what is shared, and what is never touched.

Information we collect

To provide the app's core feature — tracked-app time limits and partner accountability — we collect:

  • A minimal account profile (email, and an optional profile photo you choose to add) and your partnership status
  • The apps and categories you choose to track, and your daily time limit for them
  • Your aggregate daily usage total for tracked apps, and a per-app breakdown
  • Coarse usage in fixed 3-hour windows (e.g. "6–9pm") — not a minute-by-minute activity log
  • Whether you're under, near, or over your limit
  • Extra-time requests you send, and their approval/denial/expiry status
  • Self-unlocks (granting yourself extra time) and when they happened
  • Your notification preferences

What your accountability partner can see

Only what you leave switched on in Settings → What your partner sees, and only ever as aggregate numbers — never raw activity:

  • Your selected apps and daily limit
  • Your daily total and per-app breakdown (if both are enabled)
  • Your coarse 3-hour usage windows and current limit status
  • Requests you send them, and your shared approval/denial/self-unlock history

You control each of these independently and can turn any of them off at any time. Removing a partnership stops their access immediately.

Information we never collect

  • Messages, posts, comments, or anything you wrote
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Search terms or browser history
  • Location
  • What's on your screen, or notification content
  • Keystrokes
  • An exact, minute-by-minute timeline of your activity

On Android, the component that detects when a tracked app is opened is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent="false" — it is structurally unable to read what's on screen. It checks one thing (which app is in the foreground) against your tracked-app list, then discards everything else. On iOS, Apple's Screen Time frameworks give Receipts opaque selection tokens rather than app identities, and your Today/weekly usage report is rendered entirely inside a private, sandboxed Apple extension — Receipts cannot read, export, or upload the activity behind it. That's why iOS report totals are local-only and never appear on your partner's dashboard.

How your data is stored

Data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) using per-user access controls, so only you — and, for the specific fields listed above, your active accountability partner — can read your data. An optional profile photo is stored in a private storage bucket and is never given a permanent public URL.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Receipts is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
